About

Over the years i’ve have done lots of baking, recently it’s become my obsession. The way the deserts and sweet pastries taste and feel when you eat them is indescribable sometimes. On this site I share and express my love for the sweet treats that i’ve made. All recipes aren’t mine, but I wanted to bring appreciation for the bakers who worked hard to make these foods the way they are.


You can find the process that I used to bake these recipes, some opinions and thoughts. Enjoy scrolling through the sites as you take in the sights of the baking process. Maybe you’ll be inspired to bake them yourself! Bon Appétit!!

Baking Fun Facts!

  • The oldest oven dates back to around 6500 BC in Croatia
  • Pretzels were created by European monks in the Middle Ages
  • The largest cupcake ever made was baked in 2011 in Washington, D.C. Weighing 1,176.6 kilograms (2,594 lb) and stood over 4 feet tall.
  • Yeast is a living organism that feeds on sugars and releases carbon dioxide, causing dough to rise.
  • The Egyptians were some the first people's to develop a primitive oven called a "masonry oven."
