Blueberry Muffins

Ingredients

  • (for the crumble topping) ½ cup all-purpose flour, spooned and levelled
  • (for the crumble topping) ⅓ cup brown sugar
  • (for the crumble topping) ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
  • (for the crumble topping) 4 tablespoons cold unsalted dairy-free butter**, cubed
  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour, spooned and levelled, plus more for the blueberries
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup dairy-free Greek yogurt*
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • ⅓ cup vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups blueberries

Recipe

  1. Preheat the oven to 204°C. Lightly spray a 12-cup muffin tin or line it with muffin liners.
  2. For the crumble topping: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Use your hands or a fork to work in the butter until the mixture is crumbly. Set aside while you make the muffin batter.
  3. For the muffins: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt, sugar, eggs, vegetable oil, milk, and vanilla.
  5. In a small bowl, toss the blueberries with ½ teaspoon flour.
  6. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix. Fold in the blueberries. Use a ⅓-cup measuring cup to divide the batter among the muffin cups. Evenly sprinkle with the crumble topping.
  7. Bake for 17 to 20 minutes, or until the tops spring back to the touch and a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
