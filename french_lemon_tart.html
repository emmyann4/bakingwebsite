French Lemon Tart

Ingredients

  • 7 tablespoons dairy-free butter softened (for the tart dough)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar (for the tart dough)
  • 1 large egg (for the tart dough)
  • ½ vanilla extract (for the tart dough)
  • ¼ tsp salt (for the tart dough)
  • 1 ¼ cups all purpose flour (for the tart dough)
  • ¼ tsp baking powder (for the tart dough)
  • 3 large eggs (for the lemon curd)
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar (for the lemon curd)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest (for the lemon curd)
  • ½ cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (for the lemon curd)
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream (for the lemon curd)
  • ½ cup unsalted dairy-free butter, cut into small pieces (for the lemon curd)

Recipe

  1. In a stand mixer fit with the paddle attachment, or with an electric mixer by hand, cream the butter and powdered sugar together on medium-high speed until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  2. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and add egg. Continue mixing until combined, scraping down the bowl as necessary. Add vanilla and salt and mix until combined.
  3. Preheat oven to 176°C. On a lightly floured surface, roll out dough to a thickness of 1/8-inch to 1/4-inch. If the dough crumbles or breaks apart, press it back together with your fingertips.
  4. Loosely roll the dough around the rolling pin, then gently unroll it over the tart pan, preferably with a removable bottom
  5. Press the dough firmly into the bottom of the pan and up the sides. Trim any excess dough. Cover the dough with parchment paper or foil. Fill with pie weights or dried beans. Bake 12 minutes.
  6. Remove pie weights or beans and parchment paper or foil. Return to oven and bake until golden brown and fully cooked, about 10 to 15 minutes longer. Cool completely.
  7. To make the lemon curd: In a medium heatproof mixing bowl, place eggs, sugar, lemon zest, lemon juice, and heavy cream, if using, and whisk to combine. Place the bowl over a saucepan of simmering water. Cook on moderate heat, whisking constantly, until mixture becomes thick. It should coat the back of a wooden spoon and leave a clear pass if you run your finger through it. The curd will thicken more once cooled.
  8. Remove from heat and immediately strain mixture through a fine mesh sieve. Add butter, a few cubes at a time, and whisk until completely melted and incorporated, and mixture is smooth. Take your time with it—the whisking makes for an airy and light texture. Allow to cool to room temperature before filling the tart. (Lemon curd can be kept in an airtight container in the fridge for up to a week, or can be frozen for up to 2 months. To thaw, place overnight in the fridge. Whisk the mixture to smoothen it before using.)
  9. Fill the tart shell with lemon curd, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours until chilled. Serve with berries and whipped cream if you like. The tart is rich, so cut your servings small.
