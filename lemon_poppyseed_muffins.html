Lemon Poppyseed Muffins

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour, spooned and levelled
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 cup dairy-free Greek yogurt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ⅔ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup unsalted dairy-free butter, melted
  • ½ cup dairy-free milk
  • ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons lemon zest
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons poppy seeds

Recipe

  1. Preheat the oven to 204°C and lightly oil or spray a 12-cup muffin tin or line it with muffin liners.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt, eggs, sugar, melted butter, milk, lemon juice and zest, and vanilla.
  4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and stir just until combined. Do not overmix. Fold in the poppy seeds. Use a ⅓-cup measuring cup to scoop the batter into the muffin cups.
  5. Bake for 17 to 22 minutes, or until the tops spring back to the touch and a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean. Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
