Galette

Ingredients

  • 7 tablespoons dairy-free butter softened (for the tart dough)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar (for the tart dough)
  • 1 egg (for the tart dough)
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract (for the tart dough)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (for the tart dough)
  • 1 ¼ cups all-purpose flour sifted (for the tart dough)
  • ¼ teaspoon baking powder (for the tart dough)
  • 3 cups berry fresh or frozen (do not thaw), I like a combination of blueberries, blackberries, raspberries and chopped strawberries (for the berry filling)
  • 2 tablespoons corn starch (for the berry filling)
  • ¼ cup light brown sugar or granulated sugar (for the berry filling)
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice (for the berry filling)
  • ½ tablespoon lemon zest (for the berry filling)
  • 1 pinch salt (for the berry filling)
  • 1 large egg (for the egg wash)
  • 1 tablespoon water (for the egg wash)
  • 1½ tablespoons coarse sugar turbinado or demerara (for the egg wash)

Recipe

  1. In a stand mixer fit with the paddle attachment, or with an electric mixer by hand, cream the butter and powdered sugar together on medium-high speed until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  2. Scrape down the sides of the bowl and add egg. Continue mixing until combined, scraping down the bowl as necessary. Add vanilla and salt and mix until combined.
  3. Reduce mixer speed to low. Add flour and baking powder and blend until the dough comes together (do not over-mix). Scrape dough onto a piece of plastic wrap. Wrap tightly and chill at least 1 hour.
  4. In a large bowl, combine the berries, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, lemon zest and salt. Stir until combined. Set aside.
  5. Roll dough out on a floured surface into a 12-inch circle. Transfer dough to a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  6. Place filling in a strainer to strain any juices or use a slotted spoon to spoon the berries onto the middle of the dough (try your best to leave most of the juice in the bowl). Leave a 1½ to 2-inch border. Spread the fruit out into one even layer rather than a big heaping pile. Gently fold edges and tightly press down to seal the dough. The galette should be around 8 inches in diameter.
  7. Place the baking sheet with the galette in the freezer and preheat your oven to 176°C. If you don't have space in the freezer, chill galette in the fridge for at least 20 minutes.
  8. Once the oven's pre-heated and the crust is cold and firm, brush the edge with an egg wash (the egg mixed with water). Generously sprinkle demerara sugar over the egg wash.
  9. Bake 30-40 minutes until crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling in the middle.
  10. Allow to cool at least 10 minutes before slicing.
