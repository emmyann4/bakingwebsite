Lofthouse Cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour (for the cookies)
  • 1 ½ Tablespoons cornstarch (for the cookies)
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder (for the cookies)
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt (for the cookies)
  • ½ cup unsalted dairy-free butter softened (for the cookies)
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar (for the cookies)
  • ⅔ cup granulated sugar (for the cookies)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (for the cookies)
  • ½ teaspoon almond extract (for the cookies)
  • ¼ cup dairy-free sour cream room temperature (for the cookies)
  • ¼ cup unsalted dairy-free butter softened (for the frosting)
  • 1 cup powdered sugar (for the frosting)
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract (for the frosting)
  • Dash of kosher salt (for the frosting)
  • 2 tablespoons of plant-based heavy cream (for the frosting)
  • 1 drop gel food colouring (for the frosting)
  • ¼ cup jimmies coloured sprinkles (for the frosting)

Recipe

  1. Preheat oven to 176°C degrees.
  2. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and salt together. Set aside.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, beat butter and sugar together until combined. Add in egg, vanilla, and almond extracts.
  4. Stir in sour cream and mix until well blended.
  5. Add in dry ingredients and mix until just combined, using a rubber spatula to scrape down the sides. Do not over mix. Cover with plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator for a minimum of 60 minutes.
  6. To portion out the dough, scoop about 3 Tablespoons of cookie dough, dust hands with powdered sugar, and roll dough into a ball. 
  7. Place on parchment paper lined baking sheet, about 3 inches apart. Flatten cookies with the bottom of a glass or flat measuring cup, dipped in powdered sugar. Cookies should be between ½” and ¼” thick.
  8. Bake cookies for 9-11 minutes; rotating the baking sheet halfway through. Cookies should be just lightly golden – NOT BROWN. Cookies will continue to bake on the cookie sheet while cooling. Cool completely before adding the frosting.
  9. For frosting, beat butter for 1-2 minutes, or until it has lightened in colour and is creamy. 
  10. Add in the powdered sugar, vanilla, salt and heavy cream, a Tablespoon at a time, until the desired consistency is reached. Add food colouring to tint, if desired.
  11. Frost cookies, leaving a small rim of the cookie exposed. Top with sprinkles.
